The Golf Course

The Lowveld's championship parkland.

At the heart of the club lies our championship Par 72 golf course — eighteen holes threaded through tree-lined fairways and canal-fed water, in the heart of Zimbabwe's sugar country.

Designed by the water

Canals turned to hazards.

Eighteen holes wind across rolling, tree-lined parkland, with the estate's historic irrigation canals turned into strategic water hazards. The course is famous for greens kept immaculate year-round — a rewarding, walkable test for members and visiting golfers alike.
