The Club · Since 1960

A green oasis carved from the Lowveld.

Nestled in the heart of Zimbabwe's lush south-eastern Lowveld, Triangle Country Club is a destination where history, hospitality, sport and business come together to create unforgettable experiences — founded in 1960, inspired by one pioneer's vision.

Educational trips & tours

We love hosting school trips.

Triangle proudly welcomes school groups and educational tours — a safe, green estate where learning comes to life through sport, the outdoors and the living history of the Murray MacDougall Museum. We tailor each day, with catering, accommodation and supervision for groups of any size.
