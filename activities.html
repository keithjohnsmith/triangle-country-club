Sports Tourism

Where sport and travel come together in the Lowveld.

We are proud to promote wellness, active lifestyles and sports tourism — welcoming touring sides, tournaments, training camps and active getaways to championship facilities, comfortable chalets and warm Zimbabwean hospitality, all on one historic estate.

Sports tourism continues to drive domestic tourism growth in Zimbabwe — the influx of participants, families and supporters stimulating demand for accommodation, transport and gastronomy. Triangle Country Club is proud to champion that vision in the south-eastern Lowveld.

“Sports Tourism continues to drive Domestic Tourism growth in Zimbabwe… More than crowning champions, it showcases the power of sport to promote healthy lifestyles, strengthen networks, increase tourist spending and position our destinations as premier hosts of major sporting events that contribute to sustainable domestic tourism growth and economic development.”
— Zimbabwe Tourism Authority

School groups welcome

Educational trips & tours

A living classroom for school groups.

Triangle proudly welcomes school groups and educational tours. Our safe, green estate brings learning to life — from coaching clinics on the golf course, cricket oval and courts, to a guided visit to the nearby Murray MacDougall Museum, where the story of the pioneer who irrigated the Lowveld comes alive. We tailor each day around sport, the outdoors and history, with catering, accommodation and supervision arranged for groups of any size.
